Leading Effective Meetings

Planning a Successful Project Review Meeting

45 Minutes
4 Sections
1 Worked Example Module

Course Overview

This learning experience is designed for first-time team leaders, educators, and professionals who need to plan effective meetings.

The course focuses on a common workplace situation: planning a project review meeting. Learners will examine what makes a meeting plan effective and learn how to organize a meeting that supports clear communication, collaboration, and follow-up actions.

By the end of this learning experience, learners will be able to identify the key elements of an effective meeting plan, evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of meeting plans, and improve a weak plan using established planning criteria.

Learners will study a worked example, analyze effective and ineffective meeting plans, complete guided practice activities, receive feedback, and revise a meeting plan using a checklist of success criteria.

Estimated Time: 45 Minutes

Requirements: A web browser and internet connection. No prior knowledge is required.

Completion: Learners will complete practice activities and revise a meeting plan using the provided planning criteria.

AI Use: Learners may use course notes and provided examples. No live AI account is required to complete the learning experience.
