Leading Effective Meetings

From Agenda to Action

45–60 Minutes
4 Core Sections
1 Rise Lesson

Course Overview

This learning experience is designed for professionals who lead meetings in educational, nonprofit, government, and workplace settings.

Participants will learn practical strategies for planning, facilitating, and concluding productive meetings that result in meaningful decisions and actionable outcomes.

Estimated duration: 45–60 minutes.

Development Status: Lesson 1 has been created in Rise 360 and includes instructional content, guided practice, and feedback. Additional multimedia and accessibility supports are currently being developed.

Start Learning

Lesson 1: Writing Effective Meeting Objectives

This lesson introduces one of the most important meeting-planning skills: writing clear and actionable meeting objectives.

The lesson now includes a branching practice activity in which learners compare meeting plans, evaluate consequences, and justify their decisions. A closing assessment allows learners to identify weaknesses in a meeting plan, revise it, and explain how their revisions improve meeting effectiveness.

Learners will:

  • Identify characteristics of effective meeting objectives.
  • Compare weak and strong objective statements.
  • Practice revising a meeting objective.
  • Review feedback and model responses.
