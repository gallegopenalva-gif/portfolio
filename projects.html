Course Overview

This learning experience is designed for professionals who lead meetings in educational, nonprofit, government, and workplace settings.

Participants will learn strategies for planning, facilitating, and concluding effective meetings that produce meaningful outcomes and actionable next steps.

Estimated duration: 45–60 minutes.
