About Me

I am an educator with professional experience in Spain and the United States. Throughout my career, I have taught Technology, Computer Science, Biology, Physics, Chemistry, English, and Spanish.


I founded and managed my own online language academy and worked as an online tutor in a master's degree program, supporting adult learners in virtual learning environments.


I am currently pursuing graduate studies in Educational Technology and expanding my expertise in instructional design, e-learning development, adult learning, and workplace training.
